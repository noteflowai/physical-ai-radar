### 5. LeRobotDataset v3.0: a standardised, streamable format for robot learning data

- **Lane**: Data engine (collection / curation / scaling) ｜ **Evidence**: `[O]` ｜ **Source**: [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/lerobot-dataset-v3) ｜ `2026`

### 6. HumanEgo: zero-shot robot learning from minutes of human egocentric video

- **Lane**: Data engine (collection / curation / scaling) ｜ **Evidence**: `[R]` ｜ **Source**: [arXiv 2605.24934](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24934v1) ｜ `2026`

- **Why it matters**: It pushes the cost per new task down to minutes of data - the most direct yardstick for whether your data strategy is behind.
